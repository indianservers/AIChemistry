## Implemented curriculum organization

- Class 6–8: foundations, laboratory skills, environment, acids and bases, descriptive inorganic chemistry.
- Class 9–10: atomic foundations, stoichiometry, reaction chemistry, metallurgy and introductory organic chemistry.
- Class 11–12: physical, inorganic, organic, biochemistry and applied chemistry.
- Undergraduate: physical, inorganic, organic, analytical, biochemical, environmental and industrial chemistry.
- Postgraduate: computational chemistry, advanced spectroscopy, materials, synthesis, chemical biology, drug discovery, ADME and formulation.
