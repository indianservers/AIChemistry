# Chemistry Visualization Animation Roadmap

The animation system is split into two delivery phases. Every feature must reinforce a chemistry idea; decorative motion alone is not sufficient.

## Phase 1 — Interactive motion foundation (implemented)

1. Continuous concept-aware auto-orbit with chemistry-specific speed.
2. Drag-to-rotate with independent yaw and pitch.
3. Pinch-to-zoom with safe near/far limits.
4. Double-tap camera reset.
5. Tap-to-inspect cycling through the lesson's real chemical entities.
6. Pulsing selection halos tied to the inspected entity.
7. Seventeen chemistry motion styles, including electron transfer, lattice phonons and spectral scanning.
8. Motif-dependent motion speed for plasma, BEC, proteins, nuclei and catalysts.
9. Motif-dependent motion intensity for every visualization.
10. Perspective projection with touch-controlled pitch.
11. Depth-like atom glow, specular highlight and soft shadow.
12. Breathing atoms and nodes rather than static circles.
13. Animated scene scan line for a live-instrument feel.
14. Ambient molecular star field derived from the stable concept seed.
15. Radial energy glow behind every chemical system.
16. Cinematic fade-and-scale transitions between the five lesson views.
17. On-canvas interaction HUD for orbit, drag, pinch and tap.
18. Pause, reset and replay integrated with the reusable motion engine.
19. Stable per-subtopic animation identity across all 638 subtopics.
20. Accessibility-safe zoom limits and deterministic, non-random geometry.

## Phase 2 — Cinematic chemistry laboratory (implemented)

21. Tap-nearest-object picking instead of sequential inspection.
22. Floating callout labels connected to atoms, orbitals, peaks and apparatus.
23. Scrubbable reaction timelines with named intermediates.
24. Slow-motion transition-state and bond-breaking mode.
25. Before/after split view for structural and energetic comparisons.
26. Multi-touch atom and ligand manipulation with geometry constraints.
27. Collision bursts with conservation-aware particle trajectories.
28. Electron-flow trails and curved-arrow mechanism animation.
29. Volumetric orbital and electron-density morphing.
30. Dynamic bond-order, polarity and partial-charge overlays.
31. Crystal slicing, Miller-plane reveal and defect insertion.
32. Spectrum peak selection linked back to molecular fragments or vibrations.
33. Chromatogram and electrophoresis playback with selectable analyte bands.
34. Haptic milestones for collisions, transitions, peak selection and snapping.
35. Cinematic camera presets: overview, active site, transition state and evidence.
36. Guided narration mode synchronized with lesson explanation steps.
37. Interactive measurement probes for distance, angle, energy and concentration.
38. Challenge mode that hides labels and scores model-based predictions.
39. Snapshot comparison and shareable experiment state.
40. Performance tiers with adaptive particle counts and reduced-motion support.
